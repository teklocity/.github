# TEKlocity

### Engineering software with architectural intent, human authority, and verifiable outcomes.

**TEKlocity, LLC** develops software and practical engineering approaches for building and modernizing complex systems in the age of AI.

We believe AI can accelerate software delivery—but speed alone does not produce trustworthy systems. Architecture, engineering discipline, and accountability still matter.

## What we're building

### Archy — governed, AI-assisted software engineering

**Archy** is TEKlocity's proprietary software product, currently in active development. It is designed to help teams translate architectural intent into implementation while keeping decisions, constraints, and approvals explicit and traceable.

Its guiding principle is simple:

> **Capability is not authority.**
>
> An AI model may be capable of generating a design or writing code. That does not give it the authority to change the architecture or approve its own work.

Our approach centers on:

- **Blueprint-driven engineering** — express requirements, architectural decisions, principles, and constraints before execution.
- **Governed AI execution** — keep generated work within explicitly authorized boundaries.
- **Human accountability** — preserve review and approval for decisions that require human authority.
- **Evidence and conformance** — validate outcomes and retain the evidence needed to explain what happened.
- **Modernization with context** — respect existing systems, migration paths, and operational realities rather than treating every initiative as a greenfield rewrite.

Archy's longer-term direction also includes helping reconstruct architectural intent from existing software systems.

## Open source and reference implementations

Archy itself is **proprietary**. TEKlocity also intends to publish selected **Blueprints, technical examples, and reference implementations** as open source, where appropriate.

These projects will demonstrate engineering practices such as architecture governance, integration, modernization, testing, and AI-assisted development. Repositories will be shared here as they become ready for public use.

## Our engineering perspective

**Architecture provides direction. Engineering turns direction into working systems. Governance keeps the two aligned.**

We favor explicit decisions over implicit assumptions, reproducible results over unverifiable claims, and incremental modernization over unnecessary replacement.
